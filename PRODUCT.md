# Docket Product Brief

## One line

Docket turns an official notice into a verified next action.

## The problem

Important requirements are often spread across an email, an attachment, and an official website. A summary alone does not tell the user what to do, whether the source supports it, or whether somebody has completed it.

## The solution

Docket receives a notice, extracts the obligations, checks the relevant official source, and keeps the result as a live record. The user sees the original message, the extracted action, the deadline, the evidence, and the next step in one place.

## Primary workflow

1. Forward a notice or paste its text.
2. Review the extracted obligation.
3. Inspect the source evidence.
4. Confirm or edit the action and date.
5. Track it in the live docket.
6. Approve a follow-up draft when another person or organisation needs a reply.

## What Docket is not

Docket is not a law firm, compliance certification, government service, or autonomous agent with permission to act without review. It helps organise information and evidence. The user remains responsible for decisions and submissions.

## User roles

### Individual

Receives personal notices and wants a simple list of actions and deadlines.

### Coordinator

Tracks obligations for family members, clients, or a small operation. The first release treats ownership as a simple field. Shared permissions are a later phase.

### Reviewer

Checks evidence and edits an extracted obligation before it becomes ready.

## Product vocabulary

- Notice: the original email or pasted message.
- Obligation: a required action extracted from a notice.
- Evidence: a source excerpt or URL supporting an obligation.
- Docket: the live list of obligations.
- Follow-up: a user-approved email draft or sent message related to an obligation.

## Trust model

Every obligation should answer three questions:

1. What do I need to do?
2. By when?
3. Why does Docket believe this?

If Docket cannot answer the third question, it must show an unable to verify state rather than making a stronger claim.

## Demo narrative

The demo should begin with a realistic but fictional permit or renewal notice. It should show the notice entering, the live processing state, the extracted action, the official source evidence, the deadline in the docket, and an approved follow-up draft. The demo should use synthetic data only.
